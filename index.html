Who we are

A forum for friendship and cooperation

The OIM is an intermicronational organization founded on 12 March 2024. It provides a forum for Muslim-affiliated micronations to build relationships, exchange ideas and work together while retaining their own identities and independence.

Its foundation is described in the OIM Charter, adopted on 23 June 2024. The Charter identifies Islam's unifying role and sets out the organization's commitment to solidarity and mutual development.

Our mission

Cooperation in practice

The Charter's stated purposes include building solidarity, encouraging economic ties and celebrating shared cultural heritage.

01 / FRIENDSHIP

Friendship & solidarity

Promote goodwill, mutual respect and a stronger sense of community among Muslim micronations.

02 / DEVELOPMENT

Economic cooperation

Explore trade, economic cooperation and joint ventures that can benefit participating nations.

03 / HERITAGE

Cultural exchange

Support cultural programmes and initiatives that celebrate and preserve the heritage of member nations.

04 / SECURITY

Defence & security

Encourage diplomatic consultation and cooperation on mutual security, defence and stability issues.

05 / DIPLOMACY

Diplomatic coordination

Provide a platform for information-sharing and cooperation on micronational issues.

Membership & observer status

OIM membership is designed for eligible micronations that share the organization's values and aims.

Full membership

Open to micronations of Muslim origin that share the OIM's values and goals. Applications are subject to approval by existing members, with full members voting on membership applications.

Observer status

Micronations with at least a 20% Muslim majority may apply. Observers may observe formal proceedings but do not vote or speak in General Assembly sessions. Applications are considered by the General Assembly.

Under the Charter, each full member sends three delegates and each observer sends one. Each full member state has one vote in the General Assembly.
